# English demo with math

Inline math like $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ and bracket notation \(\ln x + \ln y\)
is supported, as well as display math:

\begin{equation}
  \hat{f}(\xi) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x)\, e^{-2\pi i x \xi}\, dx
\end{equation}

| Function | Derivative |
|----------|------------|
| $\sin x$ | $\cos x$   |
| $\tan x$ | $\sec^2 x$ |

The equation environment above becomes a real Word equation in DOCX
and is typeset by XeLaTeX in PDF.
